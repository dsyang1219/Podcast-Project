# What is publishable, and what is the story
Sept 2, 2026 — synthesis of everything in kettering_y1_test_results.md

## THE STORY (one paragraph)
Political podcasts are a major, under-measured political medium. We built a 204-show, 1.4M-passage
corpus and measured not what hosts say but HOW they address listeners. The obvious hypothesis — that
right-leaning podcasts are more hostile — is wrong: insult and profanity do not distinguish left from
right at all. What does is LISTENER-DIRECTED ADDRESS: right-leaning hosts speak to listeners in the second person
and issue more instructions — a construct that decomposition and a standard register tagger pin to
second-person address plus action imperatives (not filler, not epistemic instruction, not Biber's
persuasion dimension), and that does not move with events or topics: a trait of the host. This register is a stable property of shows, and the ideology labels
it rides on are validated three independent ways — hosts' campaign donations, expert ratings, and
the party identification of actual listeners (r = .91-.98). Turning to those listeners: people who
get their political news from podcasts, and especially from podcasts alone, are markedly more
cynical about institutions than people with any institutional source in their diet — in two
probability panels, one with exposure measured before outcome, independent of party, with informal
trust unaffected. Podcast-only diets sit at the far end of a broader non-institutional-diet
gradient. Within podcast audiences, the most listener-directed shows have the most exclusive listeners and
(exploratory) the most cynical ones; that cynicism association is direct, survives strategy framing,
populist anti-elitism and anti-media content on validated instruments, and none of those predicts
cynicism itself. The listeners are not disengaged: podcast-only diets are more civil-libertarian on
democratic norms, no more partisan, no less registered — engaged anti-institutionalism — whereas
platform-only diets carry weaker national identity, lower registration and more violence tolerance. We frame this through media-malaise / spiral-of-cynicism theory, with
mode of address as the novel candidate mechanism, and reserve causal claims for a pre-registered
holdout.

## CONTRIBUTION IN ONE SENTENCE
Partisan difference in political podcasting lies in how hosts address their audience, not how
hostile they are — and podcast-only news audiences are distinctively cynical about institutions
while being more protective of civil liberties, not more partisan, and not disengaged.

## PUBLISHABLE — in order of strength

1. MEASUREMENT + VALIDATION (methods section; the foundation)
   204 shows / 1.4M passages; validated register instruments (MFTE, syntactic imperatives).
   Show ideology validated three ways: DIME r=.68 (kappa .66, n=112); expert/editorial kappa .75-.77;
   AUDIENCE PARTY r=.978 [.948,.992] at 14 shows, .913 [.773,.981] at 22, label predicts audience
   majority party in 21/22 shows, listener-level R2=.62. DIME leg reported with its interval
   (r=.77 [.46,.99], 10 shows). Register predicts audience religiosity along the partisan divide
   (Spearman .56-.61) — reported as convergent, not independent.

2. LEFT-RIGHT REGISTER: DIRECTIVE, NOT HOSTILE (main descriptive result)
   Strongest DIME correlates: imperatives r=+.39, second-person +.39. Insult r=+.000 (p=.996).
   Insult tracks intensity (r=.55) but not direction. Replicates vs audience lean (imperatives +.41,
   insult -.16). Show-level AUC .80 vs .57 within-show. Solo/multi format handled.

3. POPULATION: NON-INSTITUTIONAL DIETS -> INSTITUTIONAL CYNICISM (main audience result; association)
   Kettering n~18,800: +0.22 SD (8-item, alpha .84), 3% attenuation across gender/race/income/
   social-media hours/platforms/urban-rural/mode/diet breadth; D +.19, R +.15.
   Gradient: each mainstream source -0.096 SD (p=2e-21); podcast-only +0.14 SD beyond platform-only
   (TOST: not equivalent). Pew ATP, probability, lagged 2-8 mo: podcast frequency -> less trust in
   national/local news (p<.0001), "main source" -0.34 SD; friends/family null; social-media trust up.
   Frame: consistent with spiral of cynicism / media malaise. Language: association, never effect.

4. REGISTER -> AUDIENCE EXCLUSIVITY (mechanism-consistent audience structure)
   Strict match, NBC excluded, listener-level, clustered: +0.13 SD per SD of register, WILD p=.0085
   (+0.15, p=.0025 broad definition), ideology-partialled. Within left-labeled shows: directive half
   59% exclusive vs 35%. Hostility does not predict exclusivity. Report; disclose 8 other audience
   measures screened.

5. PEW PARASOCIAL / AGREEMENT (audience-side mechanism signature)
   n=2,012 influencer-news consumers: podcast frequency -> personal connection (26%->39%, p=.001),
   agreement (p=.03), opinion-seeking, "helped me understand" (p<.0001); facts and "different" null.
   Holds within both parties. BH-corrected.

## EXPLORATORY — report with the search disclosed
6. REGISTER -> CYNICISM WITHIN CORPUS LISTENERS
   Strict: +0.20 SD, wild p~.003, stronger with ideology controls, all three parties, LOSO-stable,
   probability half p=.025. BUT: composite assembled from a 113-outcome scan; nothing survives BH in
   the 47-item a-priori family (min q=.094); host-name expansion kills it; not mediated by
   exclusivity (9%, CI includes 0); Q28 pre-registered text test failed. Present as exploratory with
   the family-size table. It is the pre-registered hypothesis for the holdout / Y2.
7. PARTY FAVORABILITY GAP — wild p~.05, expansion-fragile. Hedged secondary or drop.

## NOT CLAIMABLE
   "Podcasts cause cynicism." "Directive register causes cynicism." Both need the holdout or Y2.
