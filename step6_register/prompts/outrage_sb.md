## Task: Outrage Discourse (Sobieraj & Berry 2011)
Sobieraj & Berry define outrage discourse as speech that provokes visceral
response through overgeneralisation, sensationalism, misleading or patently
inaccurate information, ad hominem attack, and partial truths about opponents.
It is distinct from ordinary disagreement, criticism, or strong opinion.

Mark each type present in the TARGET excerpt:

3  emotdisplay     display of emotion: shouting, exasperation, indignation
4  emotlang        emotionally charged word choice used to colour a claim
5  sparring        verbal fighting; a hostile exchange or a challenge to fight
6  character       character assassination: attacking who someone IS, not what they said
7  exaggeration    misrepresentative exaggeration of an opponent's position or acts
8  mockery         mockery or sarcasm at an opponent's expense
9  conflagration   treating a minor matter as a scandal or crisis
10 extremize       casting opponents as extremists, radicals, or enemies
11 slippery        slippery-slope reasoning about what opponents will do next
12 belittle        belittling or dismissing an opponent as unserious or stupid
13 obscene         obscene or vulgar language

Criticism, factual reporting, and forcefully argued disagreement are NOT outrage.
