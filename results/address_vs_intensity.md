# Can address be separated from ideological intensity? (22 Sept 2026)

Address = mfte_z (reference scale). Intensity = share of political passages labelled non-moderate (inten;
distilled classifier for the 10 out-of-frame shows). r(address, intensity) = .35 across 194 shows;
r(intensity, |side|) = .87, i.e. intensity is ideological extremity regardless of direction.

## 1. Passage level, within show (150,764 passages): NOT the same thing
r(passage address, passage "takes a side") = -0.01 with show means removed. The labelling model does not read
address-heavy passages as more one-sided; the artefact reading of intensity is ruled out.

## 2. Which side does it (194 shows): address carries the directional signal
Standardized OLS, HC1: LLM side ~ address +0.32*** + intensity +0.03 (n.s.); DIME ~ address +0.41*** +
intensity -0.00; right label ~ address +0.24*** + intensity +0.22** (intensity = extremity, so it also marks
the right's more one-sided shows).

## 3. Over time (15,249 dated episodes): both are traits; intensity reacts slightly to events
Intensity ICC(1) = .46 (address .49). Event shifts for intensity: 6 January +0.15 (p .04, placebo rank .18),
2025 inauguration +0.18 (p .00, rank .09), Dobbs +0.11 (p .09); others null. Address had none. Weak evidence
that intensity is somewhat more event-responsive.

## 4. Audience, pooled 64 shows / 1,284 listeners (discovery + within-frame + out-of-frame; CR2 / wild p)
| exposure | cynicism | election distrust | education |
|---|---|---|---|
| address | +0.14 (.05/.05) | +0.13 (.05/.07) | -0.21 (.00/.00) |
| intensity | +0.32 (.01/.01) | +0.23 (.00/.00) | -0.25 (.02/.01) |
| address ǀ intensity | +0.05 (n.s.) | +0.07 (n.s.) | -0.15 (.07/.09) |
| intensity ǀ address | +0.29 (.02/.01) | +0.20 (.04/.01) | -0.18 (.10/.02) |
| address, Rogan excluded | +0.18 (.02/.03) | +0.16 (.03/.02) | -0.18 (.00/.00) |
| address ǀ intensity, Rogan excl. | +0.10 (n.s.) | +0.09 (n.s.) | -0.09 (n.s.) |
Within intensity halves (32 shows each): address predicts nothing significant in either half (low: edu -0.18
p .14; high: elec +0.11 p .09). Address x intensity interaction: n.s. on all three.
Components (535): imperatives carry election distrust (+0.17**) and education (-0.21*); second person weaker;
neither survives intensity.

## Reading
At the level of speech, address and intensity are distinct and separable: they occur in different passages,
and only address tracks ideological direction. At the level of audiences, with 64 shows, intensity is the
stronger predictor of cynicism, election distrust and education, and address adds little once intensity is in
the model (education is the closest to surviving, -0.15, p .07/.09). The paper should present Finding 1
(register differs by side) as the measurement contribution, and the audience associations as belonging to
one-sided shows, which are also the address-heavy shows; whether address contributes on its own is left to
Year 2 with both named in advance.
