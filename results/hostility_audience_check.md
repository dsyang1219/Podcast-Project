# Does show hostility predict listener attitudes? (21 Sept 2026, exploratory)

Sample: the 535 corpus listeners (385 strict + 150 within-frame), 53 clustered shows. Exposure = mean over the
shows a respondent named of the show's word-weighted lexical hostility rates (data/output/outrage_lexical.csv,
rerun 17 Sept), z-scored on the 194 corpus shows; hostility index = mean z of insults, obscenity, intensifiers,
absolutes. Controls as in the paper (party, attention, age, education except as outcome, right-wing platforms,
share of named shows leaning right). CR2 / wild p in brackets. Address = mfte_z on the reference scale.

| exposure | cynicism (8-item) | election distrust | education |
|---|---|---|---|
| hostility index | +0.09 (.44/.46) | +0.17 (.16/.16) | -0.32 (.01/.01) |
| insults | +0.05 (.56/.59) | +0.08 (.23/.15) | -0.16 (.25/.29) |
| obscenity | -0.08 (.32/.38) | -0.03 (.78/.88) | -0.06 (.57/.58) |
| intensifiers | +0.11 (.04/.06) | +0.13 (.05/.06) | -0.24 (.00/.01) |
| absolutes | +0.09 (.34/.38) | +0.17 (.16/.23) | -0.21 (.11/.05) |
| address (MFTE) | +0.16 (.03/.04) | +0.23 (.01/.01) | -0.23 (.03/.01) |
| address, hostility index controlled | +0.16 (.04/.07) | +0.21 (.02/.02) | -0.12 (.31/.37) |

Show-level r(address, hostility index) = 0.41 on 194 shows, 0.49 on the 54 audience shows.

Reading: the hostility of the shows a listener names does not predict their cynicism or election distrust;
address does, and keeps both with hostility controlled. Both predict a less-educated audience and share that
association (address falls to -0.12 n.s. with hostility in the model). Intensifiers are the one hostility
component with a marginal attitude association. Note: election distrust for address here is +0.23 against +0.18
in mfte_measure.md; the composites should be reconciled before either number is reported.
